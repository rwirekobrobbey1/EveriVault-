📋 Terms of Service & Policy

1. Freight Rate Structure

Sea freight rates are calculated on a volumetric basis ($260/m³ under 1 CBM; $255/m³ for 1 CBM and above). Air express cargo is calculated at $17/kg for general merchandise and a flat rate of ₵500 per mobile device or laptop.


2. Transit Timelines

Estimated transit times are 45–60 days for Sea Cargo and 3–7 days for Air Cargo. Transit times begin upon vessel departure or flight takeoff from China.


3. Prohibited & Restricted Items

Standalone lithium batteries, flammable liquids, weapons, and combustible materials are strictly prohibited from sea freight. Any contraband seized by customs will be the responsibility of the importer.
