Frequently Asked Questions

Operational guidelines regarding manifest clearance, volumetric parameters, and payment structures.

What is the standard transit timeframe for Air Cargo? +

Air consignments typically clear customs and arrive at our Kumasi hub within 7 to 14 working days following departure from our Guangzhou consolidation facility.

How is Sea Freight billing structured? +

Maritime freight is billed strictly per Cubic Meter (CBM) at $260/CBM. Short-term warehousing and consolidation at our Foshan facility are provided complimentary.

What are the approved settlement channels? +

Settlements can be remitted directly via Mobile Money to 0543155883 (Richmond Wireko Brobbey), via GTBank account 1304001024705, or through our automated Paystack portal.

Why is an assigned Shipping Mark mandatory? +

Your unique Shipping Mark ensures incoming packages are correctly logged and attributed to your profile among thousands of consolidated shipments entering our warehouses.
