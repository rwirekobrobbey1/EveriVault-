Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shipping with EveriVault.

How long does Air Freight take to arrive? +

Air Freight typically takes between 7 to 14 working days after departure from our Guangzhou warehouse. Updates are provided via our tracking portal.

How does Sea Freight billing work? +

Sea Freight is ideal for bulky, heavy items and takes around 45-60 days. You are billed at $260 per Cubic Meter (CBM). We provide free warehousing in Foshan for consolidation.

How can I pay for my shipping or store items? +

You can send direct MoMo payments to 0543155883 (Richmond Wireko Brobbey), direct GTBank transfer to 1304001024705, or use our Paystack automated checkout portal.

Why do I need a Shipping Mark? +

Your Shipping Mark is a unique code (e.g. GHOO1K-KWAME) that tells us exactly who owns a package when it arrives at our China warehouse among thousands of boxes. You must instruct your supplier to print this on your box.
