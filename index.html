Frequently Asked Questions

Operational guidelines regarding manifest clearance, volumetric parameters, and payment structures.

What is the standard transit timeframe for Air Cargo? +

Air consignments typically clear customs and arrive at our Kumasi hub within 7 to 14 working days following departure from our Guangzhou consolidation facility.

What is the transit timeframe for Sea Freight? +

Maritime ocean freight container consignments typically require 45 to 60 days from vessel departure at Foshan ports to customs clearance and intake at our Kumasi distribution hub.

What goods are strictly prohibited by EveriVault? +

We strictly do not accept battery goods and fragile products across our logistics lanes to prevent transit hazards and damage liabilities.

How does cargo consolidation billing work? +

Consolidation is done per request and is charged differently from the shipping cost.

What happens if my package arrives without a Shipping Mark? +

Failure to add or correctly display your assigned shipping mark on package waybills will lead to the client's own loss, as unlabelled cargo cannot be matched or tracked to your profile.
